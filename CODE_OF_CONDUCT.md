# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants should be constructive, assume good faith, and focus criticism
on the work rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when this standard is violated. Report conduct concerns privately
through the repository maintainers or the [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/JustineDevs/agent-compat/security/advisories/new)
channel when confidentiality is needed.
